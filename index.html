01 — Who we are

Whether through volunteering, partnerships, or donations, every contribution strengthens InAmigos.

InAmigos Foundation, founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization committed to creating lasting social impact.

With its base in Chhattisgarh, the foundation operates across India, addressing critical societal issues through a network of dedicated professionals and volunteers.

Registered under Section 8

Licensed by the Central Government.

80G & 12A Certified

Ensuring transparency, accountability, and tax exemptions for donors.

CSR-1 Registered

Eligible for corporate CSR partnerships.

02 — Ongoing Projects

Five missions. One shared why.

Each project addresses a specific gap our volunteers discovered on the ground. They’re active, measurable and always open to new hands.

Education

Project Shiksha

Free after-school learning centres for 400+ children from migrant and low-income families across 6 cities. Weekend volunteer teachers, donated books, curiosity-first pedagogy.

400+ learners 6 cities
Women Empowerment

Project Shakti

Menstrual hygiene awareness, sustainable pad distribution, and tailoring/skill workshops for women in semi-urban communities. Led by an all-women volunteer circle.

2,300+ women reached 60 micro-entrepreneurs
Environment

Project Prakriti

Native tree plantation drives, plastic-free campus campaigns, and climate literacy sessions with school students. Over 5,000 trees geo-tagged and tracked.

5,000+ trees 18 green schools
Rural Development

Project Gramin

Digital literacy and government-scheme access camps in partner villages. We help rural families navigate Aadhaar, banking, scholarships and agri-subsidies.

14 villages 900+ families assisted
Health & Hygiene

Project Swasth

Free health camps, eye check-ups and nutrition kits for slum communities. Partnered with local doctors who volunteer one weekend a month.

30+ camps 6,000+ screened
